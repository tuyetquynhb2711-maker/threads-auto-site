Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy describes how threads-auto ("the app") handles data. The app is a personal tool used only by its owner.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope): to create and edit one spreadsheet used as the owner's work log.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): to upload, share and delete only files and folders that the app itself created. The app cannot see any other Drive files.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used solely to run the owner's content workflow. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. The app has no server and does not send Google user data to any third party.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded videos are automatically deleted from Drive after 30 days. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and all local data can be removed by deleting the app folder.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
